Research & methods

Our recovery and cardiovascular-load indices are experimental. Research informs the inputs; our prototype weights are not clinically validated. Google and WHOOP scores are external checks, not the calculations behind our main scores.

Load

Time and intensity both matter. Training-load research compares several imperfect methods. WHOOP combines cardiovascular and muscular work with nonlinear scaling. Our first version measures cardiovascular load only, from observed heart-rate minutes above a measured resting baseline. It does not invent maximum heart rate or missing wear time.

Recovery

HRV research supports individual context, not treating one high reading as universal proof of recovery. We combine log-HRV, resting heart rate and sleep duration against your prior baseline. Missing inputs stay missing. Self-reported well-being matters too; your energy stays visible separately.

Sleep

Adult-duration consensus and individualized sleep guidance inform conservative targets. We learn from similar days and next-morning HRV/RHR, not a vendor color. Targets are observed ranges, not guaranteed sleep requirements.

Duration, continuity and timing are separate sleep-health dimensions. Wearable sleep stages are estimates: we do not prescribe universal REM/deep minute quotas. Sleep pressure and circadian timing are different; we do not claim to measure your circadian phase.

Exact prototype choices

Recovery = 50 + 12 × (0.5 × normalized log-HRV − 0.3 × normalized RHR + 0.2 × normalized sleep), clipped to 0–100. Normalization uses the prior 35 days, medians and robust spread, with 14 valid observations required. The weights and scale are our explicit design hypotheses.

Load impulse = Σ observed minutes × max(0, (HR − RHR) / RHR)². This is our prototype, not validated TRIMP. After 14 covered days a saturating 0–100 scale maps your prior 90th-percentile load to 90. Covered minutes and timestamps remain visible. Missing watch time is never filled.

Sleep targets use similar-day observations when supported. Otherwise independently above-baseline mornings supply a baseline; historical WHOOP baseline need is only a labelled fallback. Initial debt and nap adjustments are capped heuristics, and the displayed range is constrained to 7–10 hours. These limits are product guardrails, not exact biological limits.

What has and has not been validated

We test later dates using earlier data only. Vendor-reference fits never change the primary scores. Conditional next-morning evaluation uses actual obtained sleep and therefore does not prove a recommended bedtime will work prospectively. Journal associations remain exploratory. No active WHOOP subscription is needed.

No guarantee of green recovery, diagnosis, complete muscular-load measurement, or precise REM/deep staging is provided. Live updates also depend on device synchronization and verified Google authorization.
