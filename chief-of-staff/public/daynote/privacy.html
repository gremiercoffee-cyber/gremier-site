Health Journal privacy & retention

Health Journal is a private, single-owner capability of Chief of Staff. The app shell is public; journal, notes, health measurements and settings require the existing private passcode. Health credentials stay encrypted on the server in the existing Cloudflare database.

On this device, journal answers, pending sync operations, the question catalog and unsent text are stored locally. Voice recordings are kept in IndexedDB until processing succeeds or you discard them. Lock & clear this device removes these local copies and the saved passcode. Protect your device and browser profile.

When you request voice processing, audio is sent through Chief of Staff's existing transcription provider (OpenAI, or its configured Groq fallback). This server does not store raw audio. Provider retention is governed by their policies; this app cannot promise zero retention by the provider. Transcript text is sent to the configured AI model to propose observations. Only owner-confirmed observations become answers. Never-not-mentioned remains unknown.

Confirmed journal records, transcripts and Google Health measurements remain until you delete them. Deleting a transcript removes its pending proposals; confirmed answers retain their evidence quote. Delete the journal day to remove that day's answers too. Export and full Health Journal deletion are in Settings. Full deletion removes health credentials and stops future syncing, without deleting other Chief of Staff data.

WHOOP optionally imports sleep, Recovery and day Strain with read-only OAuth permissions plus offline refresh access. Its developer client secret and rotating tokens are encrypted server-side using the existing credential encryption. Disconnect stops future syncing. Export excludes credentials. The learning system uses WHOOP history as a starting prior and tentatively compares personal load and recovery percentiles across devices as Google history accumulates. It preserves raw scores and provenance, uses each provider’s own high threshold, and does not claim numerical equivalence or guarantee green recovery. Google Readiness and cardio load are entered manually while unavailable in the published API.

The Google connection requests only sleep, activity/fitness and health-measurement read permissions. It does not request location or write scopes. Disconnect deletes Health Journal's stored credentials. Because the OAuth client is shared, it does not revoke other Chief of Staff Google access; you can manage Google grants in your Google Account.

Telegram Health replies use the existing bot and only the paired owner's designated Health topic. Telegram itself can retain messages and voice recordings; deleting Health Journal data does not delete Telegram messages. Your group members may be able to read the Health topic: use only a group whose membership you trust. Health Journal does not post health information to other topics. Reminders and summaries are off by default.

Insights are descriptive associations calculated from records, with counts and missing data. They are not diagnoses, proof of cause, or advice to change medication. No synthetic health data is added to your account.

The optional Android companion test build uses an Accessibility service filtered for the Google Health package. Android grants broad screen-reading capability; the app processes only visible Google Health text and immediately discards whole-screen text. Only extracted numbers, capture time and a short diagnostic are kept in private app storage (backups disabled). No screenshot is collected. Upload requires review of the numbers and date; it sends scores, date, capture time and source, never full screen text. A hashed, revocable credential grants score uploads only. Pairing codes expire after ten minutes and work once. Use Forget pairing in the companion to clear its local data, disable its Accessibility service to stop capture, and Revoke phone access in Settings to invalidate its server credential. Full health-data deletion also revokes phone access. This test build does not promise unattended or locked-phone collection.

Personal Recovery, Personal Load and sleep targets are experimental calculations performed in code. Google/WHOOP scores serve only as separate comparison checks or explicitly labelled fallback priors. Heart-rate ingestion retains private daily minute-bin summaries, coverage, timestamps and provenance, rather than indefinite raw sample histories. These are included in health-data export and deletion. No language model invents the numerical scores. See Research & methods.
