# Focused Home

Home starts with the current context and up to three checkable tasks; more context-appropriate tasks expand explicitly. This does not change stored tasks, reminder times or notification policies. Completion remains optimistic with existing server acknowledgements/error rollback.

Between blocks, undated tasks untouched for at least 14 days with ordinary priority no longer compete for Now. Deadlines and priority 1 tasks are never aged out; an explicitly attached current-block task survives aging. Existing timing, snooze/mute and driving suitability filters still apply. Due work ranks ahead of ordinary block tasks. Full inventory remains available.

Home no longer repeats Now items in the inventory below it. Older undated/nonurgent work is retained in For later, collapsed by default; other projects, waiting items and future work are in Other open work, also collapsed. All tasks/projects remain linked. This presentation is not an automatic completion, deletion, or a claim that an old deadline stopped mattering.

Today updates is collapsed, limited to the current Jerusalem calendar day, and excludes duplicated current-block notices and task cards already in Now. Old briefings do not resurface. Area filters apply to Now, inventory and updates; mixed-area daily briefing is available only in All. Hashgacha details are collapsed; attention cards show two with remaining cards expandable. The large idle orb and persistent Home push-permission banner were removed; push controls remain in Settings, and live voice visuals still work.

Tests cover aging/protected deadlines/explicit current tasks, no inventory loss/duplicates, Jerusalem midnight, commute/time-window regressions, mobile three-item default, expansion, area filters, old-brief exclusion, closed folders, responsive layout and immediate completion during a delayed request. No production task was completed or modified for verification.
