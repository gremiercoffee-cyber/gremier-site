# Catalogue lifestyle photos

The Original Cream Latte, House Blend, and Decaf artwork was made with built-in image generation. Caramel and Vanilla use the supplied photographs. Classic, Colombia, and Sweetened Classic reuse the full-size website hero photos.

**Original Cream Latte.** Use the Original photo as the packaging reference and Vanilla and Caramel as the composition references. Make a 3:2 catalogue photo with the black four-pack carrier centered on honey oak, four 250 ml glass bottles with gold caps, warm ivory plaster, soft golden side light, and a subtle foliage shadow. Keep the Original tan label, Original flavour tag, existing packaging text, and round Gremier coffee-cup logo. Use a small group of dark roasted coffee beans at the lower left, close enough for a square crop. Remove the tray, ice, bag, cloth, keys, and other props.

**House Blend and Decaf.** Use each existing catalogue photo for the exact liter bottle and flavour label. Match the Classic or Colombia hero framing and light on a honey-oak tabletop with warm ivory plaster and a soft foliage shadow. Preserve the full tall bottle, black cap, blue House Blend or brown Decaf label, and round Gremier coffee-cup logo. Center the bottle and place an iced cold brew beside it with a few coffee beans. Keep the label clear and the glass close enough for a square crop. No added captions or flavours.
