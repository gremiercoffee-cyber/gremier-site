# Product page text: suggestions to approve

How to use: each product has a **card line** (the short text under the name in the shop) and a **description** (the product page). Edit freely and enter what you approve in the admin (Products → Edit). Facts I couldn't check are marked **[confirm]**.

Hebrew is written in the plural so it speaks to everyone.

---

## Problems in the current texts
- **No description at all:** Decaf, Sweetened Classic, Vanilla Cremier 4 Pack, The Dispenser.
- **Mini packs:** neither says the bottle size today. Both should say 4 × 250ml.
- **Classic Mini 4-Pack:** the English name says "Classic" but the Hebrew name says "קרמייר" and the Hebrew description says "4 מיני קרמל קרמייר" (caramel). Which is it? **[confirm]**
- **Vanilla syrup (Hebrew):** "למתיק" should be "להמתיק".
- **Caramel syrup (English):** "carameley" isn't a word.
- **5L Classic Dark Roast:** "subscription price" is confusing on a product that isn't a subscription.
- **Basic Coffee Bar:** the English mentions cups and straws but the Hebrew doesn't. **[confirm]** what's included.
- **Premium Coffee Bar (Hebrew):** written to one man ("פתח… אתה מוכן"). The plural reads better for everyone.
